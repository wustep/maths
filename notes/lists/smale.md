# Smale's 18 problems (1998)

Stephen Smale, *Mathematical Intelligencer* 20 (1998), 7–15; reprint in
Arnold–Atiyah–Lax–Mazur, *Mathematics: frontiers and perspectives* (AMS, 1999).
Written as a 21st-century update to Hilbert, at Arnold's IMU request.

Status cutoff 2026-08-17.
Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smale%27s_problems);
Smale's essay.

| # | Problem | Status | Note |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Riemann hypothesis | Open | Also Hilbert 8a, Clay. |
| 2 | Poincaré conjecture | Resolved (yes) | Perelman 2003, Ricci flow. |
| 3 | P vs NP | Open | Clay. |
| 4 | Shub–Smale $\tau$-conjecture (integer zeros of a univariate polynomial) | Open | Algebraic-complexity form of "P≠NP". Folder: [`problems/smale-tau`](../../problems/smale-tau). |
| 5 | Integer points on a plane curve in time $(2s)^c$ | Open | Hilbert 10 is undecidable in many variables; this asks exponential time in two. |
| 6 | Finitely many relative equilibria in the $n$-body problem? | Open | Almost all 5-body planar systems: Albouy–Kaloshin. |
| 7 | Fekete / log-energy points on $S^2$ | Open | Related to Thomson. Finite-$N$ records are computational. |
| 8 | Dynamics in general-equilibrium price adjustment | Partial | Gjerstad 2013; Lindgren 2022. No consensus that this closes Smale's ask. |
| 9 | Strongly polynomial linear programming | Open | Feasibility of $Ax\ge b$. |
| 10 | Pugh closing lemma, higher smoothness | Open | Hamiltonian surfaces: Asaoka–Irie. |
| 11 | Is 1-dimensional dynamics generally hyperbolic? | Split | (b) interval maps: Kozlovski–Shen–van Strien 2007. (a) complex / Mandelbrot: open. |
| 12 | Trivial centralizers dense in $\mathrm{Diff}^r(M)$? | Open | $C^1$: Bonatti–Crovisier–Wilkinson. $r>1$ open. |
| 13 | Hilbert's 16th problem | Open | See `hilbert.md`. |
| 14 | Lorenz attractor is strange | Resolved (yes) | Tucker 2002, computer-assisted. |
| 15 | Navier–Stokes smoothness in $\mathbb R^3$ | Open | Clay. |
| 16 | Jacobian conjecture | Split | $n=2$ still open. A 2026 claim (Alpöge, LLM-assisted) of a counterexample for $n=3$ (hence $n\ge 3$) is not a community consensus. |
| 17 | Approximate zeros of polynomial systems in average polynomial time | Resolved (yes) | Beltrán–Pardo 2008 (randomized); Lairez 2016 (deterministic). |
| 18 | Limits of intelligence | Too vague | Open-ended programme, not a yes/no. |

Later addenda (Smale): mean-value problem; Gottschalk (is $S^3$ a minimal set?); Anosov vs Franks Lie-group model.

See also the Clay list in `millennium.md` (Smale 1, 3, 15).
