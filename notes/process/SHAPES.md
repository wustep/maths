# Progress shapes (optional)

Tag a LEAVES row with at most one word when it helps. Not a second status system.
Status nouns stay **dent** and **residue** (`AGENTS.md`).

| Shape | What shrinks |
| --- | --- |
| squeeze | Bound interval (method ceilings sit beside it) |
| record | Best construction (needs a literature target) |
| ledger | Live and dead routes |
| exhaustion | Finite space (certified vs sampled stay separate) |
| coverage | Named cases, proved or refuted |
| dag | Obligations, open or discharged |

Measurement-grade is a fact about a run or artifact. Proof-grade is a theorem.
A PINN, anneal, or SAT UNKNOWN dump stays measurement until a verifier closes it.

A dead route needs a residual leaf. A method ceiling (“technique T cannot beat X”) is progress even when the published bound sits still.

Source vocabulary: [Jig progress](https://jig.so/guide/progress.md), [gates](https://jig.so/guide/gates.md). Lean optional; SAT/search/anneals stay computational.
