Simplified line $N_c<1.1185Z+4Z^{1/3}$ for all $Z\ge4$. Remark 2.6: Prop. 2.4 beats Lieb for $Z>5.3$ and Nam for all $Z\ge2$; (2.9) beats (2.8) for $Z\ge35.8$. Does not claim a $Z$-independent excess.
