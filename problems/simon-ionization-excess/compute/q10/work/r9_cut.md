# Dead line: $R\le 9$

The mass-opt identities give $Q>R/(R+1)$ on a used support of
aspect $R$. At $R=9$

$$
\min(\gamma_9,9/10)\le 0.9,\qquad 1/0.9=1.1111>1.1017.
$$

No compact $\gamma_9$ can beat the q9 printed leading $1.1017$
while the large-aspect bound stays $9/10$. q7--q9 already
recorded this wall against $1.1026$, $1.1021$, $1.1020$, and
$1.1017$.

$R=8$ binds at $1.125$. $R=9.5$ binds at $1.10526>1.1017$.
$R=9.8$ with the cut binding would print $10.8/9.8\approx 1.10204$,
which still prints as $1.1021>1.1017$. $R=9.9$ binding would
print $1.10101$, but only if a compact $\gamma$ at that split
both clears $9.9/10.9$ *and* a printed leading below $1.1017$.

A sharper large-aspect cut would reopen $R\le 9$. Chebyshev
$D\cdot M_{-1}\ge 1$ does not.
