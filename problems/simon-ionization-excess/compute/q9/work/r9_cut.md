# Dead line: $R\le 9$

The mass-opt identities give $Q>R/(R+1)$ on a used support of
aspect $R$. At $R=9$

$$
\min(\gamma_9,9/10)\le 0.9,\qquad 1/0.9=1.1111>1.1020.
$$

No compact $\gamma_9$ can beat the q8 printed leading $1.1020$
while the large-aspect bound stays $9/10$. q7 and q8 already
recorded this wall against $1.1026$ and $1.1021$; it is
stricter against $1.1020$.

$R=8$ binds at $1.125$. $R=9.5$ binds at $1.10526>1.1020$.
$R=9.8$ with the cut binding would print $10.8/9.8\approx 1.10204$,
which still prints as $1.1021$. $R=9.9$ binding would print
$1.10101$, but only if a compact $\gamma$ at that split both
clears $9.9/10.9$ *and* a printed leading below $1.1020$.

A sharper large-aspect cut would reopen $R\le 9$. Chebyshev
$D\cdot M_{-1}\ge 1$ does not.
