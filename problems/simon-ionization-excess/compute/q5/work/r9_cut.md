# Dead line — R=9 with the mass-opt cut

The mass-stationary identities force $Q>R/(R+1)$ on a used
support of aspect $\ge R$. At $R=9$ that cut is $9/10=0.9$, so

$$
\min(\gamma_9,9/10)\le 0.9,\qquad 1/0.9=1.1111>1.1057.
$$

No compact $\gamma_9$ can beat the q4 printed leading $1.1057$
while the large-aspect bound stays $R/(R+1)$. The same wall
holds at $R=8$ ($8/9$, leading $1.125$).

A sharper large-aspect cut ($Q>c>0.9045$ on aspect $\ge 9$)
would reopen this line. Two-atom mass-crit and forced-endpoint
$k$-atomic sit well above $0.92$, but that is a search, not a
cut. q4 moment notes already left the abstract $(Q,D)$ region
without a pen-and-paper improvement of $R/(R+1)$.
