## Statement

Let ES(k) be the least integer N such that every set of N points in the
plane in general position (no three collinear) contains k points in convex
position. The first unknown value is ES(7). The Erdős–Szekeres conjecture
predicts ES(7) = 33.

The current numerical record will be filled only after the theorem and
finite computation in arXiv:2512.24061 and its 2026 *Journal of
Combinatorial Theory, Series A* version have been read and replayed.

## What would count as a new bound

A dent is a complete, independently replayed finite improvement of the
published lower or upper bound on ES(7), with the strict inequality written
explicitly. A complete certificate settling a named slice of the published
finite reduction also counts only when it implies such an improved bound.

A point set must be checked in exact arithmetic for general position and
for the absence of seven points in convex position. An empty convex
heptagon (a 7-hole) is a different object and does not by itself certify a
lower bound for ES(7). A partial family exclusion, an unfinished SAT run,
or a timeout is residue, not a lower bound. This folder will not claim
ES(7) = 33 without a complete independently replayed certificate.
