# Source of the n=73 phase witness

Retrieved on 23 August 2026 from Achim Flammenkamp's
[no-three-in-line database](https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/readme.html).
The notes credit Marijn Heule with the first $n=73$ solution, found on
19 August 2026 in symmetry class `rct4`.

The exact entry came from the documented lookup endpoint with POST fields
`symm=c`, `size=73`, and `index=1`; the response reported database cut date
19 August 2026. `n73-rct4.code` is the untouched one-line encoding. It is used
only to choose SAT phases for the complete $n=75$ formula.
