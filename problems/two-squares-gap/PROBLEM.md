# A smaller constant in the two-squares gap

- Slug: `two-squares-gap`
- List: P14
- Solver: SuperGrok CLI `grok-4.6` `--reasoning-effort xhigh`
- Status: open
- Area: Number theory
- Sources: Green 100 #66; Erdős Problem #143
- Started: 2026-08-17

## Statement

It is unknown whether every sufficiently large X has a sum of two squares in [X - (1/10) X^{1/4}, X]. The elementary O(X^{1/4}) scale is known; this explicit constant is not.

## Tonight

Beat the published explicit constant, or certify a larger C such that every large X has a sum of two squares in [X - C X^{1/4}, X], with an independent verifier. A table of gaps is residue unless it produces a new infinite-family bound or a documented counterexample to a stated constant. Do not claim the Landau–Ramanujan density.
