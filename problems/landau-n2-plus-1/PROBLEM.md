## Statement

Are there infinitely many primes of the form n^2+1?

## Tonight

Do not claim infinitude. A dent is a replayable finite record:

- an independently certified list of n with n^2+1 prime, up to a stated N
- the Iwaniec P2s in the same range (at most two prime factors)
- or a prime n^2+1 that is not on the published list we cite

Search residue is not a lower bound on the number of such primes.
